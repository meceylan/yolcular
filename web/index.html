    About the data

    What is counted and how the series is made

    Active fire detections (hot pixels seen during one satellite pass, not single fires) from MODIS (since July 2002) and VIIRS (S-NPP since 2012, then NOAA-20), from NASA FIRMS, added up per calendar month (UTC) on a 0.25° grid.

    VIIRS sees several times more fires than MODIS, and the ratio is not universal: overall 3.25 in Türkiye, up to 7.07 in the Eastern Mediterranean box. So each region has its own factor for each calendar month: VIIRS total divided by MODIS total in 2012–2022 (days without data excluded). Before February 2012 the value is MODIS × factor (an estimate); from February 2012 it is the VIIRS S-NPP count itself; from November 2026 it is VIIRS NOAA-20 × 0.986 (same in all regions).

    How accurate are the estimates

    Tested by leaving each year of 2012–2022 out in turn, and independently on 2023–2025. About two thirds of months fall inside the "likely range" shown in the tooltips and charts.

    Limits

    • All values before February 2012 are estimates, rough for small drawn areas. Busy months tend to be overestimated, quiet months underestimated.
    • Months busier than any tested month are marked and probably too high (in Türkiye August–October 2009 to 2011: read the 2009 and 2011 totals as upper bounds).
    • Experimental: the Eastern Mediterranean box (few MODIS detections; about 15% of recent VIIRS detections are probably unflagged industrial sources) and the Southeast Australia box (quiet years can be about a third too high before 2012).
    • Amazon arc box: 2003 and 2004 may be up to a fifth too low (doubtful static-source flags in MODIS). Raw MODIS counts from 2023 are low (orbit drift) and are not used for estimates.
    • Boxes are land-only rectangles, not country totals. The Balkans, Caucasus and Eastern Mediterranean boxes overlap Türkiye; for Turkish territory use the Türkiye view.
    • Months with 3 or more days without satellite data are marked (small triangle) and likely too low; hatched squares have no data for the chosen series.

    Climate context

    Monthly 2 m air temperature and precipitation (NASA POWER) as the difference from the 2002–2025 average of the same month: a fire-weighted average of 5 inland points per region, placed where most detections occurred in 2012–2025 (in Türkiye: Southeast Anatolia and Çukurova, not the Aegean). Shown alongside the fire counts without any claim that one explains the other. The data was obtained from National Aeronautics and Space Administration (NASA) Langley Research Center's Prediction Of Worldwide Energy Resources (POWER) project funded through the NASA Earth Science Division. The data was obtained from the POWER Project's Monthly 2.10.0 version on 2026/11/15.
